Telling voices apart, a local model, choosing the Whisper model, sharing summaries

Captured from --demo builds of the previous commit (before) and this one (after) with PrintWindow.

Live page

  • A green Meeting summary bar appears when a meeting ends: Post to Slack (or Teams, Discord), or Email it without a webhook. Closing it sends nothing.
  • The second voice on the call reads Them 2. Names takes a list for the call side: “Alex, Sam”.

Settings page

  • Where claims are checked: Azure; Azure then a local model; or a local model only. The local choice shows Local model server and Model instead of the Azure fields.
  • Transcription: base.en (148 MB), small.en (264 MB) or large-v3 turbo (574 MB); Use the graphics card (Vulkan); Tell voices apart.
  • Chat webhook for meeting summaries (in Credential Manager) and Offer to share when a meeting ends.
