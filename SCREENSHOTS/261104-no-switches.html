Settings without on/off switches

Captured from --demo --settings before and after with PrintWindow. What the switches were set to is now how the app behaves; settings.json can still change each one.

  • Always on: look claims up, sounds and animations, listen only during calls, watch the screen, the graphics card, tell voices apart.
  • Always off: start with Windows, offer to share when a meeting ends (History's Share still posts a day's summary).
  • Left on the page: where claims are checked, the repositories, the cue volume; under Advanced, the Whisper model (default now large-v3 turbo) and the chat webhook.
